# Credits

## Artwork in the game

**The Last Light** (the painting on the east wall, and the photograph on the menu folder) reproduces
**Johan Christian Dahl, _Clouds at Sunset_** (1788–1857). The work is in the public domain; the scan is
from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johan_Christian_Claussen_Dahl_-_Clouds_at_sunset.jpg).
It is cropped to 4:3 and lightly re-toned for the gallery lighting. The attribution, title and date in
the game's wall label are fiction: the painting is a stand-in for the invented Elias Wren canvas.

## Everything else

The five gallery scenes, the witnesses' portraits, the moth brooch, the gallery mark, the folder, the
paper and the board are original SVG and CSS drawn for this project. No photography, stock imagery or
fonts are loaded at runtime.
